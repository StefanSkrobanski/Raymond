Raymond

How many times light may bounce on its way to the camera. Glass needs plenty.

Blurs what's out of focus. It focuses on what you last tapped.

More clears the image faster, but makes moving the camera less smooth.

Smooths the grain while the image is young, keeping edges and textures sharp. It fades out as the samples build up.

While the camera moves, draws a quick, rough image at a lower resolution (as low as your device needs to keep up), then the full one when it stops.

Dims the rare very bright specks that take ages to average out, at a small cost in accuracy.

Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers or right-drag to pan, tap something to orbit round it (and move closer), double-tap to go back.
